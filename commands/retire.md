description: Retire a promise because the behaviour is meant to change
argument-hint: "<number> <why it should change>"

The human wants to retire a thisisfine promise: $ARGUMENTS

The first word is the promise number; the rest is the reason. If either is missing, run `thisisfine status` (full CLI command in your session context), show it, and ask which promise and why.

Run `thisisfine retire <number> --reason "<reason>"`, relay the question it prints word for word, and end your turn. Nothing is retired until the human replies `y`.
